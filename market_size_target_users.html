Target Enterprise Buyer & User Personas

The key operational decision-makers who evaluate, authorize, and utilize ECMS across corporate directorates.

⚙️

Chief Operating Officer (COO)

Executive Buyer • Operations Oversight
Core Operational Friction

Sluggish cycle times across regional offices; no visibility into where multi-million-naira expenditure requests and capital requisitions are stalled.

ECMS Strategic Value

Real-time command center dashboard, 48h/96h automated SLA timers, and an 80% reduction in end-to-end turnaround time.

💻

Chief Information Officer (CIO)

Technical Buyer • IT Architecture
Core Operational Friction

SaaS license sprawl across ticketing, form builders, and cloud storage (SharePoint, AWS); vulnerability to dollar-exchange-rate devaluations.

ECMS Strategic Value

Unified architecture combining workflow, shift rostering, and sovereign drive storage (cDrive) in local currency at 80% lower TCO.

⚖️

Chief Risk Officer / Head of Audit

Governance • Regulatory Compliance
Core Operational Friction

Approvals occurring over unmonitored WhatsApp and email threads; missing physical paperwork; severe risk during statutory and ISO audits.

ECMS Strategic Value

Immutable digital file jackets, unalterable chronological minute trails, and tamper-proof forensic audit logs capturing user IP and timestamps.

📍

Field Operations Director

Tactical Execution • Workforce Management
Core Operational Friction

Technicians assigned tasks blindly without skill verification; unmonitored shift rosters; no verified photographic evidence of completed site work.

ECMS Strategic Value

Skill-based dispatch (e.g. COREN engineer certification), working shift windows (8h rotational), and mandatory mobile photo checklists with GPS tags.
