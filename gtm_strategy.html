Enterprise Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

Commercial execution playbook targeting multi-facility private enterprises, engineering contractors, financial institutions, and logistics operators seeking to replace fragmented software stacks.

🏢 1. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

Enterprises characterized by high multi-site coordination, strict regulatory reporting, and a high volume of internal expenditure clearances.

Tier 1: Multi-Location Financial Services & Telecoms Banks, micro-finance networks, and telco infrastructure operators managing hundreds of branch facilities, ATM repair work orders, and internal audit controls.
Tier 2: Engineering, Construction & Infrastructure Multi-site contractors (e.g. Julius Berger, Craneburg) requiring formal engineering dossier sign-offs, COREN engineer matching, and heavy machinery shift dispatch.
Tier 3: Manufacturing, Energy & Logistics Industrial syndicates managing plant maintenance, transport rosters, vendor invoice clearances, and supply chain quality sign-offs.

👔 2. Target Enterprise Decision-Makers

Messaging tailored directly to C-suite operational and risk priorities.

Chief Operating Officer (COO) / Head of Operations Value Hook: "Eliminate operational black holes. See exactly which regional manager is stalling CapEx approvals, and guarantee 48-hour resolution across all field maintenance tickets."
Chief Information Officer (CIO) / CTO Value Hook: "Slash enterprise workflow licensing spend by 80%. Consolidate ticketing, form builders, and document storage into one secure, sovereign cloud platform."
Chief Risk Officer (CRO) / Head of Internal Audit Value Hook: "Achieve 100% forensic auditability. Every minute, delegation of authority, and expenditure clearance is stamped with an immutable cryptographic log."

🚀 3. Land-and-Expand Sales Motion

Frictionless entry point that expands naturally across all enterprise directorates.

01
Land: Single High-Friction Department (Weeks 1-4)

Deploy ECMS to solve one acute operational pain point: Facilities Maintenance, Fleet Dispatch, or Procurement Requisitions.

02
Demonstrate: Immediate Turnaround Time Reduction (Month 2)

Show executive leadership that cycle times dropped from 14 days to 48 hours with zero lost paperwork.

03
Expand: Cross-Directorate Orchestration (Months 3-6)

Expand licenses to Corporate Memos, Executive Approvals, Legal Contracts, and Multi-Shift Field Operations.

💰 4. Packaging & Commercial Terms

Commercial packaging engineered to win against dollar-denominated multinationals.

Local Currency Sovereign Billing (₦ / Local FX) Shields corporate IT budgets from foreign exchange currency devaluations, guaranteeing stable annual software expenditures.
Zero Add-On Storage Model All packages include native Connected Drive storage (cDrive), eliminating separate monthly cloud storage invoices.
Turnkey Implementation Guarantee Fixed-fee, 30-day onboarding with guaranteed user training and pre-configured organizational organograms.
