ECMS Core Feature #1 "Inter-MDA Forms"

Website Pitch: "Send a request to another MDA by filling a form... track via ticket ID reference for funds and quotes."

Cross-Subsidiary & Inter-Departmental Service Requisitions

In a commercial conglomerate (e.g. Dangote, BUA, Stanbic, Access Bank), regional business units submit structured CapEx, legal review, and logistics clearances to Group Treasury or Shared Services. Unique tracking tokens eliminate lost emails and guarantee accountability.

Vs. ServiceNow / Jira: Jira requires expensive Atlassian Marketplace plugins; ServiceNow requires heavy multi-tenant architecture licensing. ECMS provides out-of-the-box cross-entity routing.

ECMS Core Feature #2 "Gov Drive & Custody"

Website Pitch: "Safeguard documents with Gov Drive. Say goodbye to lost physical documents and enjoy secure access."

Sovereign Enterprise Drive (cDrive) with Zero Cloud Add-On Billing

Provides integrated document storage right inside the ticket dossier with granular RBAC, version control, and document previewing. No need to provision external AWS S3 buckets or Microsoft SharePoint sites with secondary monthly licensing.

Vs. Microsoft Power Platform / Salesforce: Microsoft Dataverse charges $40/GB/month and Salesforce charges $125/GB/month for extra storage. ECMS includes native drive storage with zero storage penalty tax.

ECMS Core Feature #3 "e-Signature Integration"

Website Pitch: "Facilitate electronic signing, collaborate with multiple signers on a single document, and enjoy seamless integration."

Executive Dual-Authorization & Delegation of Authority (DOA)

Enforces corporate dual-authorization on procurement orders, executive board memos, and banking vendor contracts. Replaces costly standalone DocuSign/Adobe Sign subscriptions ($40/user/month saved) with an integrated legal-grade e-sign modal.

Vs. Jira / Kissflow / Zoho: Standalone form tools require separate DocuSign/HelloSign integrations. ECMS embeds browser-based cryptographic e-signing directly inside the case workflow.

ECMS Core Feature #4 "Escalation Management"

Website Pitch: "Keep teams on track with automatic notifications for overdue tasks, ensuring tasks reach the right authority."

Statutory 48h / 96h Corporate SLA Watchdog & Re-routing

Active countdown watchdog enforcing operational SLAs. Delays at 48 hours trigger notifications to line supervisors; stalled requests at 96 hours automatically reassign to executive directors, preventing business bottlenecks.

Vs. Jira / Monday.com: Typical project management tools only send passive emails. ECMS actively re-routes ownership up the corporate organogram to prevent deadlocks.

ECMS Core Feature #5 "Dashboard & KPI View"

Website Pitch: "Track Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) providing actionable data for operational improvement."

Turnaround Time (TAT) Analytics & Directorate Bottleneck Heatmaps

Gives the Chief Operating Officer (COO) and Board of Directors real-time visibility into operational velocity across regional branches, average time-to-clearance, and contractor SLA compliance rates.

Vs. ServiceNow: ServiceNow requires specialized Performance Analytics licensing and weeks of BI dashboard configuration. ECMS delivers turnkey queue TAT metrics out of the box.

ECMS Core Feature #6 "Resource Efficiency"

Website Pitch: "Optimize resource allocation across agencies, ensuring tasks and projects are completed efficiently."

Technician Skill Matching & Shift Capacity Rostering

Translates corporate approvals into field work orders, matching technician professional certifications (e.g. COREN engineering licenses, Bar credentials) and working shift schedules (8h rotational, 24/7) with mobile GPS photo proof.

Vs. Kissflow / Jira / Zoho: Pure form and ticketing tools have zero concept of physical technician skill certifications or shift balancing.
