The 3 Hidden Licensing Traps of Legacy Incumbents

Why enterprise buyers routinely face 2x to 4x budget overruns after signing legacy workflow contracts.

⚠️ The Mandatory Implementation Tax

ServiceNow and Salesforce cannot be deployed out of the box. Organizations are forced to engage certified implementation partners billing $180–$350/hour. If the partner fails or departs, the system becomes an unmaintainable customized monolith.

⚠️ The Cloud Storage & Database Squeeze

Platforms like Microsoft Dataverse and Salesforce cap initial file and relational storage at low thresholds (e.g. 10GB). For multi-facility operations uploading thousands of high-resolution inspection photos and CAD drawings, storage penalty invoices quickly exceed the base license cost.

⚠️ The Module Fragmentation Creep

Vendors bait enterprise buyers with low ticketing rates, but hide essential capabilities behind separate SKUs: Field Service, Shift Rostering, E-Signature, and Advanced SLA Reporting all require additional premium per-seat charges.
